Intelligence Methodology

OSINT / CSINT Workflow

A structured intelligence process focused on collecting, verifying and analyzing digital information.

Phase 01 — Collection
Gathering information from open sources, archives, social platforms and digital environments.

Phase 02 — Verification
Cross-checking information between multiple sources to improve reliability.

Phase 03 — Entity Analysis
Identifying connections between accounts, organizations, locations and digital traces.

Phase 04 — Relationship Mapping
Building connection graphs and analyzing links between different entities.

Phase 05 — Reporting
Creating structured intelligence reports with timelines, findings and conclusions.
